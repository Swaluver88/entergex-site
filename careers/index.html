02 / Who we look for

People who finish things.

Titles matter less to us than what you have made and how you made it.

A

You sweat the last ten percent

The ripple in a puddle. The half-second a menu takes. You notice, and it bothers you until it's right.

B

You check your own work

You run it on the real device, read the numbers, and say plainly what passed and what didn't.

C

You're curious about the subject

Games, aviation, weather, simulation. You don't have to fly, but you should want to know why bank angle matters.

D

You treat players and pilots well

No dark patterns, no data grabs. You would rather earn trust slowly than spend it fast.
