About

A small studio with a long horizon.

EnterGex makes games and flight simulation tools. We are independent, we are small on purpose, and we would rather ship three things we are proud of than thirty we are not.

01 / The studio

Games and flight simulation, under one roof.

They look like different worlds, but they ask for the same craft. A tower defense game and a turbulence map both live or die on simulation you can trust, an interface that explains itself, and a hundred details nobody asked for but everyone feels.

That is the work we like. Maze Sniping generates its battlefields and a galaxy of 50,000 worlds. RTurbSim turns raw g-load from pilots' simulators into a live map. SimPayloads takes the arithmetic out of loading an aircraft.

EnterGex was previously known as EnterMax. Same studio, same products, new name.
