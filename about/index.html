Company

An independent software company. Until recently, EnterMax.

What we are

EnterGex makes games, flight simulation software and the tools around them.

From outside that looks like an odd mix. From inside it is one skill used several ways: take something complicated, model it accurately enough that people can rely on it, and then make it disappear behind an interface that explains itself.

We are independent and small, and both are deliberate. Nobody outside the company decides what we build or when it is finished. Some of what we are building has been announced. A good deal has not.

The name

EnterMax is now EnterGex.

If you knew us as EnterMax, this is the same company with the same work. Anything that still carries the old name is ours.

The mark

A hexagon, opened.

The hexagon

The most efficient way to divide a plane into equal cells. It is also, literally, how RTurbSim divides the sky.

The star

The hexagon is cut open as a doorway, and there is something on the far side of it. That is the promise we would like every product to keep.

The orbit

One moon, always moving. On the home page it really does go round.
