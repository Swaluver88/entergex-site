Frontier defense

A tower defense game on generated industrial battlefields. Armored drones march along a winding service track, and you decide where your cannon turrets stand. Then take the fight across a galaxy of 50,000 drone worlds.

How it plays

  • Every outpost run generates a new battlefield with a guaranteed enemy route.
  • Start with one free turret and choose its position; a coverage ring shows exactly what it will reach.
  • Destroy drones and clear rounds to earn credits for more turrets.
  • Hold out through four rounds, then bring down the Drone King, a towering six-legged boss with a crown of spires that enrages as it weakens.
  • Earn up to three stars for each victory, and coins to spend on cosmetic turret finishes.

Drone Universe

  • Explore a spiral galaxy of 50,000 drone worlds, from the calm rim to the lethal core.
  • Every world shows its toughness and how many rounds it holds, and a crown marks the worlds ruled by a Drone King.
  • Eight kinds of world, each with its own ground, light and weather: dunes, glaciers in snowfall, magma flats raining embers, toxic swamps under acid rain, crystal fields, storm worlds, rust deserts and glowing abysses.
  • Swift drones, swarms and armored drones join the fight.
  • Liberate worlds to keep your best stars and earn coins, then head for the next world on the way to the core.

Screenshots

Weather that changes the scene

  • Clear skies, overcast days, or full storms, with rain falling across the whole map.
  • Wet ground fills with puddles that ripple and reflect your turrets, drones, and lightning.
  • Thunderclouds drift past, and lightning strikes the outskirts.

Made for your phone

  • Play in portrait or landscape; the battlefield and controls adapt to how you hold your phone.
  • Choose Full, Balanced, or Battery graphics.
  • Haptic feedback and Android's back gesture.
  • No ads.

Your account and your data

Every part of the game works as a guest, with no account. Signing in with Google is optional and shows your name in the game.

If you sign in, Google gives the game your basic profile: your name, email address, profile picture and account ID. The game checks Google's sign-in token on your device, keeps only your display name, and discards everything else, including the token. Nothing is sent to EnterGex.

Signing out removes your name. To erase everything the game keeps on your device, including coins and the worlds you've liberated, open Settings, choose Account, then Delete my data.
