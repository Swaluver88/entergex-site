Simulation

Software for people who fly at a desk and take it seriously. We pull live telemetry out of Microsoft Flight Simulator and X‑Plane and turn it into something a pilot can act on.

Tools and the web

On finishing

Maze Sniping was run on a real Galaxy S24+ until it held 60 frames a second on worlds full of snow, embers and acid rain. It did not count as done before that.

On measuring

RTurbSim grades turbulence from the spread of vertical g over a rolling window, corrected for bank angle, because a steep turn is not rough air.

On your data

Maze Sniping keeps what it knows on your phone. This website runs no scripts and sets no cookies, so we could not tell you were here if we wanted to.
