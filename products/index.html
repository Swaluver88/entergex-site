Maze Sniping

A tower defense game on generated battlefields. Place cannon turrets along a winding track, bring down the Drone King, then carry the fight across a galaxy of drone worlds.

  • 50,000 worlds to liberate, from the calm rim to the lethal core
  • Rain, lightning and puddles that reflect the fight
  • Portrait or landscape, with no ads

02Flight sim toolFree

SimPayloads

A free weight and balance calculator for flight simulators. Work out your load before you push back, so the aircraft you fly is the aircraft you planned.

  • For Microsoft Flight Simulator, Prepar3D and X-Plane
  • Online at simpayloads.com, with nothing to install
  • Free to use
simpayloads.com →
03Flight sim toolEarly Access

RTurbSim

Real-time turbulence detection for flight simulators. Pilots' simulators report the bumps they feel, and a live map shows everyone the turbulence before they fly through it.

  • A live, color-coded map built from pilot reports
  • For Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024, and X-Plane 11 and 12
  • Rewind the map up to 48 hours
rturbsim.com →
