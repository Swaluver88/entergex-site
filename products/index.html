Maze Sniping

A tower defense game on battlefields that are generated fresh each run. You place cannon turrets along a winding track, bring down the Drone King, and then take the fight across a spiral galaxy of drone worlds, from a calm rim to a core that is not calm at all.

RTurbSim

Simulator weather turns rough without warning, and no forecast knows what your simulator is actually rendering. Other pilots' simulators do. RTurbSim reads the bumps out of each aircraft and puts them on a shared map, so you can see rough air before you are in it.

Kind
Simulation
Runs on
A Windows app beside Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024 or X‑Plane 11 and 12, with the map on the web
Status
Early Access

SimPayloads

A realistic flight starts with knowing what the aircraft weighs and where that weight sits. SimPayloads does the arithmetic for simulator pilots, so the aircraft you take off in is the one you planned.
