03 / Fly

See the bumps before you reach them.

Simulator weather can be rough without warning, and no forecast knows what your simulator is actually rendering. Other pilots' simulators do.

Measured in the cockpit

RTurbSim's Windows app reads vertical g-load from the simulator and corrects for bank, so a steep turn doesn't read as turbulence.

MAP

Reports are binned into hexagonal cells and graded light, moderate or severe, with the last 30 minutes shown and up to 48 hours to rewind.
